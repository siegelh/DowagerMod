This leaderhead is a combination of a converted model from Atlantica Online and a leaderhead of Civ4. Therefore, while some modifications of the models and artwork were made by me, I take no credit for both and all rights are within NDOORS Corporation and Firaxis.

This leaderhead uses the Cyrus animations. Some of the animations had to be modified slightly to fit the new model. It's completely modular. All files in the zip-file are already put into the necessary folders, unzipping it directly in the Mods folder of BtS should result into a working mod. The leaderhead has a polycount of about 6700 triangles. There are a noshader and a shader version included.
