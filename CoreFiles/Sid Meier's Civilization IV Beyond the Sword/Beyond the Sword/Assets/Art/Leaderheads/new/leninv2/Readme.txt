Vladimir Lenin For Civ4
================================================================================
A shaded Lederhead for the Leader of the Great Revolution.

He is based on the idea for the Lenin LH by Dutchking, who also gave some creative 
input. Also parts of Dutchkings textures were used.

Installation
================================================================================
This is not a playable Mod, just artwork.

My Art-Define for him:

		<LeaderheadArtInfo>
			<Type>ART_DEF_LEADER_LENIN</Type>
			<Button>Art/LeaderHeads/Lenin/Lenin_button.dds</Button>
			<NIF>art/LeaderHeads/Lenin/Lenin.nif</NIF>
			<KFM>art/LeaderHeads/Lenin/winston_churchill.kfm</KFM>
			<NoShaderNIF>art/LeaderHeads/Lenin/Lenin_noshader.nif</NoShaderNIF>
			<BackgroundKFM>art/LeaderHeads/Lenin/winston_churchill_BG.kfm</BackgroundKFM>
		</LeaderheadArtInfo>

More information and about adding stuff to the game at CivFanatics.
http://forums.civfanatics.com/forumdisplay.php?f=177
