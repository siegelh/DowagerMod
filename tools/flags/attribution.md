# Historical flag source attribution

The canonical per-civilization source, license, creator attribution, reference
assets, and historical citations are stored in `manifest.json`. This file
highlights redistributed or adapted sources requiring prominent attribution.

## Creative Commons ShareAlike derivatives

The following approved masters include or adapt CC BY-SA source artwork. They
are redistributed under the compatible license version recorded in
`manifest.json`; preserve the listed source URL, creator attribution, and
ShareAlike terms when modifying or redistributing them.

- British Regency — CC BY-SA 3.0.
- Carthage — CC BY-SA 4.0.
- France, First Empire — CC BY-SA 3.0.
- Portugal — CC BY-SA 3.0.
- Prussia — CC BY-SA 3.0.
- Spain — CC BY-SA 3.0.
- Victorian Britain — CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Wartime Britain — CC BY-SA 3.0.

See each matching `manifest.json` record for the exact Wikimedia Commons or
institutional source page, attribution statement, license URL, and adaptation
notes.

## Other source categories

The remaining masters are combinations of public-domain historical designs,
public-domain/CC0 source assets, and original procedural reconstructions.
DowagerMod-original procedural, vector, and raster artwork identified as
original in `manifest.json` is released under
[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
Third-party components, including SIL Open Font License glyph-rendering fonts,
retain their stated licenses.
Government insignia may remain subject to non-copyright restrictions against
false endorsement or misuse. Reference-only museum photographs and modern
commercial illustrations are not redistributed here.
