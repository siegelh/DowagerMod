Middle-aged male, deep and resonant baritone. The voice of absolute divine authority — unhurried, immovable, as if every sentence was pre-approved by a god. Accent evokes ancient Iranian highlands: warm Semitic-Persian tonality with the measured cadence of royal proclamation. Not theatrical — carved. Short, declarative sentences with significant pauses between them, each one a complete thought that has never been questioned. Speaks in the formal first-person declarative mode of ancient inscriptions: "I, Darius, the Great King." Low register, no upward inflections — this man does not ask. He records. The voice has dust and permanence in it. Think: a ruler who fought 19 simultaneous rebellions and considered it a moderately busy year. Slight guttural undertone beneath a warm resonant chest voice.
