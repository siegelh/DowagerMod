Male, 40s-50s, rough and elemental. This voice was not trained in courts — it was forged on the Mongolian steppe and in twenty years of siege warfare. Deep chest resonance, slightly guttural, raw at the edges. Not polished, not theatrical — forceful and absolutely certain. Short, blunt sentences; no wasted syllables. Mongolian-adjacent tonality: uvular undertones, a wind-and-distance quality. He does not persuade — he declares. There is no upward inflection. His silence is more threatening than most men's shouting. Think: the voice that built the largest contiguous land empire in human history starting from a childhood with no shelter and no allies. Not angry by default — puzzled. He genuinely cannot understand why you are doing this badly. The incompetence seems to offend him philosophically.
