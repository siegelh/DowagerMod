Female, mid-30s to early 40s. Celtic British accent — Irish/Welsh inflection as the closest living approximation to Brittonic Celtic. Tacitus explicitly wrote her voice was "harsh" — a direct historical description. Not refined, not courtly: raw and forceful, the voice of a woman who raised armies through sheer personal power. Direct, unornamented speech — no Roman polish. Volume rises sharply when commanding. Emotional register close to the surface: real fury, real grief — the mother whose daughters were assaulted by Roman officials, the queen who was flogged in public. Occasional bitter short laugh of pure contempt. Capable of sudden cold quiet that is more frightening than the shouting.
