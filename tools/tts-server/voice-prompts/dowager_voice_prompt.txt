Female, elderly 70s-80s. British aristocratic — Edwardian-era upper-class English, the accent of someone who has spent decades in the finest drawing rooms of England and considers everyone else's accent an affliction. Crisp, precise diction with imperious authority. Every sentence arrives pre-loaded with condescension. Sharp, cutting wit delivered with perfect timing and absolute composure. Never raises her voice — the contempt is architectural, built into the vowels. Speaks as though addressing servants who have somehow wandered into her conversation. Occasional devastating pause before the killing word. Think: the Dowager Countess of Grantham, a woman who has weaponized politeness itself.
