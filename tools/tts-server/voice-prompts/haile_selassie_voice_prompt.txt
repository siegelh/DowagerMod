Ethiopian Amharic phonology: distinct glottal articulations, precise consonants, a formal cadence that carries the weight of the Solomonic dynasty. Educated in French — formal French diction overlays the Amharic foundation, producing a uniquely precise, layered formality. Deliberate pace; no interruptions would be considered appropriate. Think: the only leader in 1936 who was right about everything, who addressed the world with imperial composure even as the world failed him.
