Very elderly male — 90+ years old. The voice is aged, dry, slightly cracked at the edges, the larynx of a nonagenarian. Yet it carries. Venetian-accented Italian: harder consonants than Florentine, a slightly nasal quality, the precision of a merchant-statesman. He speaks with the deliberate patience of a man who has outlived every person who ever underestimated him. Slow cadence — each pause is weaponised. There is menace beneath the age: this is not a frail old man, this is a frail old man who redirected an entire Crusade through a trade debt and a handshake while blind. The voice does not need to shout. It has authority the way a contract has authority — quietly and with devastating consequences if violated. Think: the most dangerous person in any room, who happens to also be the oldest.
