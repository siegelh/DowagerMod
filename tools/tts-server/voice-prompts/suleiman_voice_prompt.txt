Male, 40s-60s. Ottoman sultan at the height of empire: rich, educated, courtly baritone with an Istanbul-Ottoman flavor when speaking English. Tone majestic, composed, intelligent, and faintly amused. He is both lawgiver and poet, so delivery should balance imperial command with lyrical elegance. Measured pace, flawless diction, no wasted words. Threats are velvet-sheathed; praise is formal; wit is dry. He should sound like a man accustomed to being obeyed by continents.
