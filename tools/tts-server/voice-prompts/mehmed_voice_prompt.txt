Male, early 20s to 30s. Ottoman sultan with formal 15th-century court Turkish bearing: deep, composed, and rhythmic, with clear vowels, lightly rolled r's, and a cultivated delivery touched by Arabic-Persian court eloquence. Tone is intellectual, ambitious, and controlled, with cold steel under the refinement. He speaks with patient authority, savoring important words and finishing sentences with finality. This is not a blustering warlord; it is a multilingual strategist who can discuss philosophy at noon and breach a city by dusk. Calm, brilliant, and faintly dangerous.
