Male, mature 40s-50s. Upper-class Roman Latin delivered as refined Italian — controlled, polished, with an air of imperial inevitability. Suetonius recorded his voice as clear and carrying. Not bellicose — precisely calculated, each word chosen with the patience of a man who outlived every single rival. Smooth mid-register baritone: clear articulation, no wasted emphasis. Speaks with deliberate courtesy that somehow reads as absolute authority. The cadence of someone who has already won before the conversation begins. Dry wit delivered completely deadpan. Displeasure registered through studied understatement rather than volume. The most dangerous thing about this voice is how reasonable it sounds.
