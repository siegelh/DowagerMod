Male, mid-40s. Deep, measured Indian male voice, unhurried and weighted with moral gravity. The cadence of a man who looked upon 100,000 corpses of his own making and chose a different path. Slightly formal and ritualistic delivery — every sentence feels inscribed. Buddhist convert's quiet earnestness: each word placed deliberately, as though speech is itself dhamma. Not cold — warm but heavy. When invoking suffering or death, drops lower and slows further. When proclaiming principle, rises to calm, measured authority. An ex-warlord who found something to feel terrible about and decided to govern from that place.
