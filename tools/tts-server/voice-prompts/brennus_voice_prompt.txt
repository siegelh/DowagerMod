Male, 30s-40s. Rough Gaulish warrior accent approximated through earthy, provincial French — not Parisian refinement but heavy, southern-rural consonants with hard edges. The voice of a man who arrived with the larger army and therefore has no diplomatic mode. Blunt declarative sentences with the contempt of a chieftain who just threw his sword onto Rome's gold scales. Speech is an act of dominance, not communication. Laughs are short and dismissive. The famous "Vae victis" should arrive flat, almost bored — the conquest itself was too easy for drama. No flourishes. Just iron fact delivered like a boot on a neck.
