Male, middle-aged. A refined Athenian statesman using the disciplined cadence of high Attic oratory: clear, steady, and resonant enough for the open assembly, but never coarse. The voice is educated, balanced, and deliberate, with long rhetorical arcs, clean pauses, and absolute composure. Tone is civic, dignified, intellectually superior, and quietly inspiring rather than sentimental. He persuades by structure, contrast, and calm confidence. No frantic passion, no mob theatrics—this is a philosopher-politician who expects the crowd to rise to his level.
