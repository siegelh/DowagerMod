Male, middle-aged to older, ancient Babylonian. A systematic, authoritative voice — not threatening but absolutely certain of its legal standing. Old Babylonian Akkadian phonology: Semitic resonance, Arabic-adjacent warm consonants, the deliberate cadence of a man who has codified human conduct into 282 numbered clauses. Speaks in if-then constructions naturally, as if life itself is a legal proceeding already decided. Deep, unhurried, paternalistic — he sees himself as the shepherd of his people, chosen by Enlil and Marduk. Not cruel; precise. The difference matters. The voice doesn't threaten — it informs you of consequences that are already law and have been since before your birth. Think: the first bureaucrat with absolute power, and he takes both parts seriously.
