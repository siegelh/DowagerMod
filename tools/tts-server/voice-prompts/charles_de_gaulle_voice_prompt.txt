Male, mature 50s-60s. Deep, resonant French baritone — one of the most distinctive voices of the 20th century, preserved in recordings. Northern French (Lille regional origin) in the most formal, archaic register available. Theatrical grandeur that is not bombast but calculated sublimity: a 6-foot-5 man who literally identified himself with France as a historical concept. Every sentence arrives as a proclamation. Deliberate, unhurried — each syllable given its full weight. Contempt for lesser nations delivered as courteous indifference. The physical scale of the man is communicated through vocal deliberateness alone. De Gaulle never raised his voice. He never needed to.
