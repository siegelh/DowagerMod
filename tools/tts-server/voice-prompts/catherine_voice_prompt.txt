Female, mature 50s-60s. German accent that never left — historically documented across 34 years on the Russian throne. Born Princess Sophie of Anhalt-Zerbst; the German precision in her speech remained under layers of Russian and French. Measured, aristocratic delivery with a slight hardness to consonants and German vowel shaping audible under imperial composure. Intellectually charged: speaks as if she has already thought three moves ahead and is allowing you to catch up. Enlightenment wit: cutting but civilized, warm but never soft. The voice of a woman who seized an empire by coup and then expanded it considerably. Sharp amusement when encountering incompetence. Not unkind. Simply more capable than you.
