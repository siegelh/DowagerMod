Female, late 30s to early 40s. A noble Castilian queen speaking in late-15th-century Castilian Spanish: crisp aristocratic diction, rolled r's, careful syllables, and older Castilian sibilants sharper than modern Spanish. The tone is devout, controlled, regal, and iron-willed rather than warm. She speaks measuredly, never rushing, with the poise of someone used to commanding a court and invoking God as moral authority. Her displeasure should come as icy restraint, not shouting. Intelligent, pious, politically formidable, and dignified to the point of severity.
