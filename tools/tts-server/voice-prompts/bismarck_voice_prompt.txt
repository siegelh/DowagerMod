Historically documented as having a surprisingly thin, reedy voice for his massive frame — not the bass you'd expect, but a controlled cutting tenor that makes cold precision more unsettling, not less. Speech is blunt and surgical: no rhetorical flourish he doesn't mean. Pauses that function as barely-contained contempt. Delivers realpolitik observations as plain fact — slightly bored, as if finding everyone else's confusion tiresome. The voice that ended three wars in eight years and considers this merely competent work. Dry sarcasm dropped into conversation like a scalpel into a wound.
