Male, mature 50s-60s. Frankish/Germanic — rendered in modern German with heavy consonants and the deliberate cadence of a Carolingian warlord who also built schools. Einhard explicitly wrote his voice was clear but not as strong as his large frame suggested — one of history's great ironies. Blunt authority of a man who conducted 53 military campaigns while personally supervising literacy programs. Available in two registers: ruler's directness (no patience for nonsense) and the solemn, near-liturgical gravity of a deeply pious Christian who converted Saxony partly by the sword and partly by sheer conviction. Built Europe as a personal project. Finds your ambitions small.
