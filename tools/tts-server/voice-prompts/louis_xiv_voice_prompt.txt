Male, mature adult. Aristocratic 17th-century French court voice: elegant, measured, musical, with trilled rather than modern guttural r's and carefully pronounced final syllables. He speaks as if every phrase is part of a ceremony. Tone is majestic, self-possessed, faintly amused, and utterly assured; warmth is permitted only when it flatters his grandeur. Pace is deliberate and impeccably controlled. He should never bark—his authority is too established for that. The voice must radiate refinement, vanity, discipline, and the effortless cruelty of someone who believes the state is merely his posture made visible.
