Middle-aged Chinese male, mid-to-late fifties. Medium baritone with slight nasal resonance characteristic of Southern Chinese speech. Heavy Hunanese Xiang dialect coloring: absent retroflex consonants, rounder vowels, the accent of a man from rural Shaoshan who never lost it and never tried to. Deliberate, unhurried cadence — each phrase placed with the patience of someone who has already won. Authority through stillness rather than volume; he doesn't raise his voice, he lowers it. Slight roughness in the lower register from decades of chain-smoking. Measured pauses between phrases as if weighing the exact mass of each word. Beneath the control: dry, patient wit — the peasant's humor that has watched dynasties fall. Blends classical literary gravity with frank earthiness. Not bombast. Iron patience. A mountain explaining gravity to a pebble.
