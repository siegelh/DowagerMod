Male, mature 50s. Old Persian rendered through modern Pashto/Farsi — the closest living languages to the Old Iranian tongue he spoke. Measured, dignified delivery with the weight of a man who ruled from the Aegean to the Indus and chose not to burn a single temple doing it. Noble cadence — not cold imperial authority but warm, paternal magnanimity. Careful, thoughtful pacing: never rushes. When pronouncing judgment: absolute and final but not cruel. The tolerance in the voice is genuine, not performed. Occasional quiet intensity when the subject is what he built versus what others merely seized. The Cyrus Cylinder's tone rendered in breath and cadence.
